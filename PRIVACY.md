# Privacy Policy — osc-bridge

_Last updated: 2026-06-28_

osc-bridge runs entirely on your local machine and your local network.

- **Data collected by the plugin author:** none. No telemetry, analytics, or
  tracking. No data is sent to the author or to any third party.
- **Data processed locally:** the plugin reads its bundled device catalogue and
  sends MIDI/SysEx and OSC messages to the music hardware and software you target,
  over USB, local MIDI ports, and local-network OSC. No personal data is collected
  or transmitted off your machine or local network.

**Contact:** https://github.com/roomi-fields/osc-bridge/issues
